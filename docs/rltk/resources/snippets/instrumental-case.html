Instrumental Case – кем/чем (твори́тельный паде́ж)

  1. “by” (a person, e.g. written by him) or “with” (a tool, e.g. written with a pen)
  2. Indicating in a season or in a part of the day (in the summer = лéтом; in the morning = ýтром)
  3. Some adjectives require instrumental, e.g. (не)довóльный, зáнятый, богáтый, оби́льный, etc.
  4. Many verbs, e.g. control, being/becoming, moving body parts, roles(”as”), and passive verbs
  5. Verb complements: he made us happy; we call them "memes"; they considered him a fool.
  6. 6 prepositions:
    1. с (with)
    2. за (behind)
    3. мéжду (between)
    4. над (above)
    5. пéред (in front of)
    6. под (under)
  7. Feminine endings can change ~ой/ей to ~ою/ею (especially poetry/lyrics/literature): новою книгою.
