Accusative Case – кого́/что (вини́тельный паде́ж)

  1. Direct Object of most verbs (I saw him; We told you that; She read the article)
  2. Duration/Frequency (We read [for two years]/[every week] = Мы читáли [два гóда]/[кáждую недéлю])
  3. Distance covered (They drove for five miles = Они проéхали пять миль)
  4. 10 prepositions (The hallmark of most of these is motion/change/passage):
    1. в (into)
    2. на (onto)
    3. за (for)
    4. чéрез (across/through/over)
    5. под (under)
    6. про (about)
    7. по (up to [certain point in space/time inclusive])
    8. с (approximately)
    9. о(б) (against [a solid surface])
    10. сквозь (through, especially when difficult)
