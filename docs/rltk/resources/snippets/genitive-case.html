Genitive Case – кого/чего (роди́тельный паде́ж)

  1. “of” (This is also how you say “’s”, e.g. John’s car = car of John)
  2. After the following words expressing quantity: мáло – little/few, мнóго – many/much/a lot of, немнóго – not many/not much/a few/a little, нéсколько – a few/some/several (note the stress), скóлько? – how many/how much?, стóлько – that many/that much/so many/so much
  3. After some cardinal numbers
  4. Lack or absence (usually with нé было/нет/не бýдет or with verbs negated with не)
  5. Renders the word than after short-form comparative adjectives: лучше его (better than him)
  6. Indicating on a certain date, e.g. on the fourth of July = четвëртого июля
  7. Many verbs require genitive, including verbs of fearing/achievement/expectation/absence
  8. > 40 prepositions:
    1. без (without)
    2. вместо (instead of)
    3. вне (outside)
    4. возле (by/near)
    5. вокруг ([a]round)
    6. для (for)
    7. до (before)
    8. из ([out] from)
    9. из-за (because of)
    10. крóме (except)
    11. мимо (past)
    12. напротив (opposite)
    13. насчëт (regarding)
    14. óколо (around)
    15. от ([away] from)
    16. помимо (besides)
    17. пóсле (after)
    18. прóтив (against)
    19. ради (for sake of)
    20. с ([down] from)
    21. среди (among)
    22. у (at)
    23. etc.
