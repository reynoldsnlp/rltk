Case: Instrumental (кем/чем)

Uses

  1. “by” (agent) or “with” (instrument).
  2. Time/season (лéтом, ýтром).
  3. Adjective government: (не)довóльный, зáнятый, богáтый, оби́льный, etc.
  4. Verb government: control, being/becoming, movement, roles (“as”), passive verbs;
    • Complements: he made us clean; we call them prophets; they considered him a fool.
  5. Prepositions (6): с, над, под, пéред, мéжду, за.
  6. Feminine endings: ~ой/ей → ~ою/ею (poetry/lyrics/literature): новою книгою.
