Case: Nominative (кто/что)

Uses

  1. Dictionary form of nouns and adjectives.
  2. Subject of the sentence (who/what is doing the verb?).
  3. Predicate with a form of “to be” (e.g., I am a teacher).
  4. Calling someone’s name (vocative use).

Note: Some nouns have a vocative form: бог → бóже, госпóдь → гóсподи, отéц → óтче (prayer/scripture only). Names in ~a can lose the ~a: мам, Саш, Коль, Вань, Кать.
