Nominative Case – кто/что (имени́тельный паде́ж)

  1. Dictionary form of nouns and adjectives
  2. Subject of the sentence (subject = who/what is doing the verb?)
  3. Predicate of a sentence (In English, it follows a form of “to be”, e.g. I am a teacher)
  4. Used to call someone’s name to get their attention (this is called “vocative”)
    1. Some nouns have a separate vocative case: бог(бóже), госпóдь(гóсподи), отéц(óтче(in prayer/scripture only)). Names in ~a can lose the ~a: мам, Саш, Коль, Вань, Кать.
