Prepositional Case – ком/чём (предлóжный паде́ж)

  1. Used only with prepositions:
    1. на (on/at)
    2. в (in/at)
    3. о(б) (about)
    4. при (in presence of/at time of)
    5. по (upon/after)
  2. Locative: Some masculine nouns have a special prepositional form in ~у́ when referring to location, e.g. в лесу́ (in the forest) vs о ле́се (about the forest).
